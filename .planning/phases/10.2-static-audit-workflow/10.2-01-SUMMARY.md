See `key-decisions` in frontmatter. One material deviation from the plan sketch: the plan did not
originally anticipate correcting the audit outcome model mid-phase — this was added after determining the
Phase 10.1 draft's PASS/WARN/FAIL enum did not match the full specification's explicit outcome/severity/
confidence separation, and fixing it now (before Phase 10.3 builds more rules on top) was cheaper than
fixing it later.
